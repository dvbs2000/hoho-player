常见问题

怎样把 2D 视频变成 3D？
打开一个 2D 视频的预览，选择「AI 3D」（把 2D 视频实时变成 3D）。想先体验一下，可以点首页「试试样例」里的「2D 视频」。没有订阅时，每个视频可以免费试看 10 秒。
哪些视频可以用 AI 3D？
H.264 或 HEVC 编码、最高 6K、最高每秒 60 帧、最高 10 位色深的 2D 视频。超出这个范围的视频仍可以普通播放；已经是 3D 的视频不需要 AI 3D。
支持哪些视频？
MP4 和 MOV；视频为 H.264/HEVC、音轨为 AAC 的 MKV（其他音轨的 MKV 播放时没有声音）。立体格式支持左右（SBS）和上下（OU）3D、VR180、360° 3D（左右或上下），以及空间视频和 Apple 沉浸视频。非 3D 的 360° 视频需要是 Apple Projected Media 格式。暂不支持 AVI、WMV、WebM、TS/M2TS/MTS 等格式。
怎样连接 NAS？
在首页点「网络」，再点「添加服务器」，输入 NAS 的地址、用户名和密码（SMB）。要在局域网里查找，点「更多」（…），选择「查找网络服务器」。
提示需要「本地网络」权限？
AIVU Player 需要「本地网络」权限才能查找和连接 NAS。请打开「设置」，前往「隐私与安全性」>「本地网络」，然后打开 AIVU Player。
播放 NAS 上的高码率视频会卡？
6K、8K 等高码率视频需要更快的网络。建议让 Vision Pro 使用 5 GHz 或 6 GHz Wi-Fi，NAS 用网线连接路由器。
画面位置不合适？
AI 3D 播放时，拖动画面下方的横条可以移动画面；左右或上下格式的 3D 视频直接拖动画面。点「重置」让画面回到正前方；VR180 和 360° 视频会重新对准你的视线。
怎样取消订阅或恢复购买？
订阅可以随时在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中取消。换了设备或重新安装后，在 App 的购买页面点「恢复购买」。
怎样把诊断文件发给我们？
在 App 的「设置」>「诊断」中点「准备导出诊断文件」，再点「分享诊断文件」，用邮件发到上面的地址。只有你主动分享，文件才会离开设备；文件里记录了什么，请见隐私政策。

FAQ

How do I turn a 2D video into 3D?
Open a 2D video's preview and choose "AI 3D" (turns 2D video into 3D in real time). To try it first, tap "2D Video" under "Try the Samples" on the home screen. Without a subscription you can preview each video free for 10 seconds.
Which videos work with AI 3D?
2D videos in H.264 or HEVC, up to 6K, up to 60 frames per second and up to 10-bit color. Videos outside this range still play normally; videos that are already 3D don't need AI 3D.
Which videos are supported?
MP4 and MOV, and MKV with H.264/HEVC video and AAC audio (MKV files with other audio tracks play without sound). For 3D: side-by-side (SBS) and over-under (OU) 3D, VR180, 360° 3D (side-by-side or over-under), spatial video and Apple Immersive Video. 360° video that is not 3D needs to be in Apple Projected Media format. AVI, WMV, WebM and TS/M2TS/MTS are not supported yet.
How do I connect my NAS?
On the home screen, tap "Network", then "Add Server", and enter your NAS address, user name and password (SMB). To search your local network, tap More (…) and choose "Find Network Servers".
The app asks for Local Network access?
AIVU Player needs Local Network access to find and connect to your NAS. Open Settings, go to Privacy & Security > Local Network, and turn on AIVU Player.
High-bitrate NAS videos stutter?
6K and 8K videos need a faster network. Use 5 GHz or 6 GHz Wi-Fi on Vision Pro, and connect the NAS to your router by cable.
The picture is not where I want it?
In AI 3D, drag the bar under the picture to move it; for side-by-side or over-under 3D, drag the picture itself. Tap "Reset View" to bring it back in front of you; VR180 and 360° video re-center on where you are looking.
How do I cancel or restore a subscription?
Cancel anytime in your Apple Account subscription settings. On a new device or after reinstalling, tap "Restore purchases" on the app's purchase screen.
How do I send you a diagnostic file?
In the app, go to Settings > Diagnostics, tap "Prepare diagnostic export", then "Share diagnostic file" and email it to the address above. The file leaves your device only when you share it; see the Privacy Policy for what it contains.
