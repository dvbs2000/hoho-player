常见问题

怎样把普通 2D 视频变成巨幕 3D？
打开任意 2D 视频的预览，选择「AI 3D」（在窗口中实时转 3D）或「3D 沉浸」（隐藏周围房间，在 100%–500% 微曲环抱巨幕中观看实时 3D）。想先体验效果，可以点首页「试试样例」里的「2D 视频」。未订阅 Hoho Pro 时，内置样例可免费完整播放，你自己的每个视频均可免费完整以 2D 播放，并享有前 2 分钟的 3D / AI 3D 免费试看。
如何调节 AI 3D 的立体深度强度和屏幕亮度？
在沉浸播放控制栏或「设置」中，可以在「柔和 / 标准 / 深邃」三档 AI 3D 景深强度之间实时切换，并可在 40%–100% 范围内调节沉浸屏幕亮度。
支持哪些视频、音频与字幕格式？
支持 MP4、MOV、MKV、M4V、WMV（无 DRM 加密）、AVI、TS、M2TS、WebM 等常见容器；支持 HEVC (8/10-bit)、MV-HEVC、H.264（含隔行扫描与高于 4K 视频）、VP9、AV1、WMV1/2/3、VC-1 视频，以及 AAC、AC-3、E-AC-3、FLAC、ALAC、Opus、MP3、WMA、PCM 多声道音频。立体格式支持左右（SBS）、上下（TB）全宽与半宽 3D、180° VR180、360° 全景、Apple 空间视频和 Apple 沉浸视频（AIVU）。字幕支持直接提取切换 MKV 内嵌文本字幕（SRT / ASS / SSA / WebVTT）、MP4/MOV 内嵌字幕，以及自动匹配同名或 Subs/ 子目录中的外挂字幕。
怎样连接 NAS、Emby 或 Jellyfin？
在首页点「网络」，再点「添加服务器」，可选择添加 SMB、WebDAV、Emby 或 Jellyfin 服务器。要在局域网里自动搜索，点右上角「更多」（…）并选择「查找网络服务器」。通过 WebDAV（HTTPS）下载视频到本地离线库时，即使摘下 Vision Pro 也会在后台继续完成传输。
提示需要「本地网络」权限？
Hoho 3D 需要「本地网络」权限才能查找和连接局域网内的 NAS 或私人影院服务器。请打开系统「设置」，前往「隐私与安全性」>「本地网络」，然后开启 Hoho 3D。
躺下看片时怎样对齐画面、怎样防止手部误触弹窗？
当仰视角度超过 35° 开始播放或连播切集时，画面会自动贴合你的天花板视线；坐姿与躺姿的画面高度偏移会分别独立记忆。点击「重置视角」会将画面重新居中对齐当前视线，同时保留你调好的 100%–500% 巨幕缩放比例。若想避免手部微动唤醒控制栏，可在沉浸控制栏或「设置」中开启「双击唤醒控制面板」。
播放 NAS 上的高码率 8K 视频会卡？
6K、8K 等超高码率原盘视频需要稳定的局域网带宽。建议让 Vision Pro 连接 5 GHz 或 6 GHz Wi-Fi（尽量靠近路由器），并将 NAS 用千兆/万兆网线连接路由器。
怎样取消订阅或恢复购买？
年度订阅包含 3 天免费试用，可随时在系统「设置」的 Apple 账户订阅管理中取消（在试用期结束前至少 24 小时取消不会产生扣费）。更换设备或重新安装后，可在 App 的「设置 > Hoho Pro > 方案与购买」页面点「恢复购买」。
怎样把诊断文件发给我们？
在 App 的「设置 > 诊断」中点「准备导出诊断文件」，再点「分享诊断文件」发送到上面的邮箱。只有你主动分享，文件才会离开设备；详情请见隐私政策。

FAQ

How do I turn a 2D video into giant-screen 3D?
Open any 2D video's preview and choose "AI 3D" (real-time 3D in a window) or "Immersive 3D" (hides your room and floats a 100%–500% curved wraparound 3D cinema screen). To try it first, tap "2D Video" under "Try the Samples" on the home screen. Without Hoho Pro, built-in samples play in full for free, and your own videos play in full in 2D for free with a 2-minute free 3D / AI 3D preview per video.
How do I adjust AI 3D depth strength and screen brightness?
In the immersive control bar or Settings, you can switch between 3 AI 3D depth presets (Gentle / Standard / Deep) and adjust immersive screen brightness from 40% to 100% in real time.
Which video, audio, and subtitle formats are supported?
Supports MP4, MOV, MKV, M4V, WMV (unencrypted), AVI, TS, M2TS, and WebM containers; HEVC (8/10-bit), MV-HEVC, H.264 (including interlaced and >4K), VP9, AV1, WMV1/2/3, and VC-1 video; and AAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, FLAC, ALAC, Opus, MP3, WMA, and PCM audio. For 3D & VR: Full/Half Side-by-Side (SBS) and Over-Under (TB) 3D, 180° VR180, 360° panoramic video, Apple Spatial Video, and Apple Immersive Video (AIVU). Subtitles support embedded MKV text tracks (SRT / ASS / SSA / WebVTT), embedded MP4/MOV tracks, and automatic external subtitle matching (including Subs/ folders).
How do I connect my NAS, Emby, or Jellyfin server?
On the home screen, tap "Network", then "Add Server" to connect via SMB, WebDAV, Emby, or Jellyfin. To scan your local network automatically, tap More (…) and choose "Find Network Servers". WebDAV (HTTPS) downloads continue in the background even after you take off your Vision Pro.
The app asks for Local Network access?
Hoho 3D needs Local Network permission to discover and stream from your NAS or home media server. Open system Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network, and turn on Hoho 3D.
How do I watch while lying down without accidental panel pop-ups?
When playback starts while looking upward past 35°, the screen automatically aligns to your ceiling gaze, and sitting vs. reclined height offsets are saved independently. Tapping "Reset View" recenters the screen to your gaze while keeping your 100%–500% zoom. To prevent slight hand movements from waking the UI, enable "Double-tap to show controls" in the immersive bar or Settings.
High-bitrate 8K NAS videos stutter?
6K and 8K high-bitrate streams require fast local Wi-Fi. Connect Vision Pro to 5 GHz or 6 GHz Wi-Fi and wire your NAS to the router via Ethernet.
How do I cancel or restore a purchase?
The annual subscription includes a 3-day free trial and can be canceled anytime in your Apple Account subscription settings (cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged). On a new device or after reinstalling, go to Settings > Hoho Pro > Plans and purchases and tap "Restore purchases".
How do I send a diagnostic file?
In the app, go to Settings > Diagnostics, tap "Prepare diagnostic export", then "Share diagnostic file" and email it to the address above. The file leaves your device only when you share it; see the Privacy Policy for details.
