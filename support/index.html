常见问题

怎样把 2D 视频变成 3D？
打开视频预览，选择「可缩放沉浸」（普通视频 AI 变立体）。没有订阅时，每个视频可以试看 10 秒。
支持哪些视频？
MP4、MOV、MKV 等常见格式；左右（SBS）和上下（OU）3D、VR180、360° 以及空间视频。
怎样连接 NAS？
在「网络服务器」中点「添加服务器」，输入 NAS 地址和账号。App 只连接你添加的服务器。
播放 NAS 上的高码率视频会卡？
6K、8K 等高码率视频需要更快的网络。建议让 Vision Pro 使用 5 GHz 或 6 GHz Wi-Fi，NAS 用网线连接路由器。
画面位置不合适？
拖动画面下方的横条可以移动画面；点「重置」回到正前方。
怎样取消订阅或恢复购买？
订阅可以随时在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中取消。换了设备或重新安装后，在 App 的购买页面点「恢复购买」。

FAQ

How do I turn a 2D video into 3D?
Open the video preview and choose "Scalable Immersive" (it adds AI depth to 2D). Without a subscription you can preview each video for 10 seconds.
Which videos are supported?
Common formats such as MP4, MOV and MKV; side-by-side (SBS) and over-under (OU) 3D, VR180, 360° and spatial video.
How do I connect my NAS?
In "Network Servers", tap "Add Server" and enter your NAS address and account. The app connects only to servers you add.
High-bitrate NAS videos stutter?
6K and 8K videos need a faster network. Use 5 GHz or 6 GHz Wi-Fi on Vision Pro, and connect the NAS to your router by cable.
The picture is not where I want it?
Drag the bar under the picture to move it; tap "Reset View" to bring it back in front of you.
How do I cancel or restore a subscription?
Cancel anytime in your Apple Account subscription settings. On a new device or after reinstalling, tap "Restore purchases" on the app's purchase screen.
