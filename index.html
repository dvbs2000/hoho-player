AI 实时 2D 转 3D + 500% 环抱巨幕

无需提前转码，在设备端将普通 2D 电影、番剧、演唱会、家庭录像实时转为自然舒适的立体 3D。开启「3D 沉浸」自动隐藏现实房间，支持 100%–500% 线性无级缩放、三档景深强度（柔和 / 标准 / 深邃）与 40%–100% 屏幕亮度调节。

专为「躺平看片」打磨的零打扰操控

仰视超过 35° 自动将巨幕贴合天花板视线，坐姿与躺姿高度独立记忆；重置视角保留画面缩放；支持开启「双击唤醒控制面板」防止手放被子上误触弹窗，并在沉浸空间内提供选集抽屉与进度条缩略图预览。

8K VR180、360° 全景与原生空间视频

全制式原生支持 Apple 空间视频 (MV-HEVC)、Apple 沉浸视频 (AIVU)、180° VR180、360° 全景以及全宽/半宽左右 (SBS) 与上下 (TB) 3D 视频，自动识别并支持播放器内一键手动切换与左右眼互换。

NAS 私人影院直连 + 摘下头显后台下载

内置 SMB (SMB2/3)、WebDAV、Emby 与 Jellyfin 客户端，支持观看进度条与「继续观看」横幅。WebDAV (HTTPS) 下载在摘下 Vision Pro 后依然后台续传；直接读取 MKV 内嵌文本字幕 (SRT/ASS/SSA/WebVTT) 与外挂字幕。

Real-Time AI 2D-to-3D + 500% Curved Cinema

Convert standard 2D movies, anime, concerts, and home videos into comfortable stereoscopic 3D on device in real time. Enter "Immersive 3D" to hide your room, scale smoothly from 100% to 500%, and customize 3 AI depth presets (Gentle / Standard / Deep) plus 40%–100% screen brightness.

Built for Lying Down & Zero Distraction

Looking upward past 35° automatically aligns the cinema screen to your ceiling gaze, with independent height memory for sitting and lying down. Enable "Double-Tap to Show Controls" to prevent accidental hand wakeups, and switch episodes directly from the in-headset playlist drawer.

8K VR180, 360° & Native Spatial Video

Native playback for Apple Spatial Video (MV-HEVC), Apple Immersive Video (AIVU), 180° VR180, 360° panoramic video, and Full/Half Side-by-Side (SBS) & Top-Bottom (TB) 3D—with automatic detection and instant manual override inside the player.

Direct NAS Streaming + Headset-Off Downloads

Built-in SMB (SMB2/3), WebDAV, Emby, and Jellyfin clients with watch progress bars and a "Continue Watching" banner. WebDAV (HTTPS) downloads continue in the background after taking off Vision Pro. Supports embedded MKV text subtitles (SRT/ASS/SSA/WebVTT) and external subtitles.
