AIVU Player 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 25 日
提供者：Jing Leng（AIVU Player 开发者）

概要

AIVU Player 不收集、不存储，也不分享你的任何个人数据。App 没有账号，没有广告，没有统计或跟踪，开发者也没有接收数据的服务器。

App 在你的设备上使用的数据

以下数据只在你的设备上使用，不会发送给我们或任何第三方：

  • 照片图库：经你授权后以只读方式读取视频，用于显示和播放，不会修改或上传。
  • 你选择的文件和文件夹：只访问你主动选择的位置。
  • 局域网与 NAS：只连接你自己添加的服务器（SMB），用于浏览和播放你的视频。视频直接在你的设备和 NAS 之间传输。服务器地址和登录信息只保存在本机（登录信息在系统钥匙串中）。
  • AI 3D 转换：完全在设备上运行，使用 App 自带的模型，画面不会离开你的设备。
  • 设置和播放进度：例如续播位置，保存在本机。
  • 技术日志：为了排查问题，App 在本机保存一份运行日志（例如播放事件和错误）。日志不包含你的视频内容或登录信息，也不会被发送出去。

购买

订阅和购买由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们收不到你的付款信息。App 通过 Apple 的 StoreKit 确认你的购买状态。Apple 如何处理这些数据，请见 Apple 的隐私政策。

第三方

App 不包含任何第三方分析、广告或跟踪组件，也不与任何第三方分享数据。

数据保留与删除

所有数据都保存在你的设备上：

  • 删除 App 会移除它保存的设置、播放进度和日志。
  • NAS 登录信息保存在系统钥匙串中，在 App 中「移除服务器」时会一并删除。如果要删除 App，建议先移除服务器。

我们不持有你的任何数据，所以无需另行申请删除。如有疑问，欢迎联系我们。

儿童

App 不以儿童为对象，也不收集任何人的个人数据。

变更

本政策如有变更，会在本页更新，并修改生效日期。

AIVU Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026
Provider: Jing Leng, developer of AIVU Player
Contact: sdicfund@hotmail.com

Summary

AIVU Player does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no developer server that receives data.

Data the app uses on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Photos library: with your permission, videos are read (never modified or uploaded) to show and play them.
  • Files and folders you choose: only the locations you select.
  • Local network and NAS: the app connects only to servers you add (SMB), to browse and play your videos. Video streams directly between your device and your NAS. Server addresses and sign-in details are stored only on your device (sign-in details in the system keychain).
  • AI 3D conversion: runs entirely on your device with models included in the app; no frame leaves your device.
  • Settings and playback positions: for example where to resume, kept on your device.
  • Technical log: to help troubleshoot, the app keeps a log on your device (for example playback events and errors). It contains no video content or sign-in details and is never sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. The app confirms your purchase status through Apple's StoreKit. For Apple's handling of this data, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking components and shares no data with any third party.

Retention and deletion

All data stays on your device:

  • Deleting the app removes its settings, playback positions and log.
  • NAS sign-in details live in the system keychain and are deleted when you choose "Remove Server" in the app. Remove your servers before deleting the app.

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing to request. Questions are always welcome.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
